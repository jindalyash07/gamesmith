- (difficulty_not_increasing) Scale difficulty through a single parameter that is monotonic in solution length, such as more pieces, a larger grid, more required steps, or fewer free moves. Do not change several parameters at once, because they can offset each other and make a later level easier.
- (difficulty_not_increasing) Do not hand-author levels or randomly generate them without checks.
